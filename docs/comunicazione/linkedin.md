## Main post (EN)

If you thought running a tech event was a walk in the park, here's a demo. 🎟️

It's 9:52am. The keynote speaker can't find the entrance, their laptop only has USB-C, the Lab Wi-Fi just died, 49 people are queuing at check-in and the staff chat has 47 unread messages. One is from my mom: "Did you eat?"

For anyone who runs tech events, that's a normal Saturday... After years of GDG events I turned it into a game: OVERBOOKED! Survive the DevFest.

First comes the Countdown, with a few free evenings and too much to do: whatever nobody takes on happens anyway. Then the day, with lost speakers, silent projectors, queues, a dog in the room and a gremlin named Murphy. Last, the retro, like after a real event.

Six events, from a Thursday meetup to a night hackathon to the DevFest, and every tip cites a source: these are lessons organizers learned the hard way. There's also a weekly challenge, the same day for everyone, with a Chapter Cup between chapters (the server replays every game to verify it).

Gemini is an extra, off by default. Turn it on with your own key, which stays in your browser and goes only to Google, and the game writes cards, chats and the local newspaper for your community and your city.

If you don't run events, it's a glimpse of what goes on behind every talk that starts on time. If you do, play it with your team and tell me your worst crisis: it becomes a card in the game.

Free, in the browser, in five languages: https://overbooked.web.app/?s=li
